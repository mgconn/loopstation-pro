Shortcuts

  • 1–8 select track
  • Space record → play → overdub → play
  • O overdub
  • P play track
  • S stop track
  • Z undo layer · Y redo
  • Shift+C clear track
  • M mute · L solo · R reverse · H half-speed
  • Enter play/stop all
  • T tap tempo · K metronome
  • W record mix on/off
  • ? this help · Esc close
